## 1. The training lab (docs/TRAINING.md made real)

All commands live under `python -m transcripts_ai.lab ...`.

| Layer | Commands | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Measurement | `export-records` `tally` `split` `make-bank` `score` | Human decisions become versioned training records; session-level train/eval splits (frozen sessions never train); deterministic scoring; the memory-only baseline every model must beat |
| 2a Teacher panel | `teachers` `panel` | GPT / Claude / Gemini / local Llama vote on review items; unanimous verdicts past a deterministic evidence gate bank as training data; everything else queues pre-answered |
| 2b Overnight loop | `overnight` | One crash-safe command: panel → bank → queue → scorecard → morning report. Checkpoints every item; resume never re-pays; out-dir locked |
| 3 Compile | `compile` | Records → SFT chat data + DPO preference pairs (human decisions only), axolotl/unsloth format, leak-checked splits |
| 4 Train kit + gate | `train-kit` `promote` | Generates the RTX-3080 QLoRA script, Ollama Modelfile and runbook sized to the dataset; `promote` admits a model only after it beats the baseline on the same bank |
| 5 Proof + growth | `time-travel` `quiz` | Memory-compounding measurement, and post-game recap trivia whose every answer is a training label |
| Gold transcripts | `whisper-prompt` `gold-score` `names-check` | Seed Whisper with campaign names; score machine vs hand-corrected transcripts (WER + name accuracy) and mine every fix as a label |

## 2. Engine additions

- **Vault → memory sync** (`python -m transcripts_ai vault-sync`): reads any
  campaign's Obsidian folder — entities, kinds, and frontmatter `aliases`
  lists become human-approved aliases. One-way; the vault is never written.
- **Official 5e knowledge** (`transcripts_ai/data/dnd5e_names.txt`, ~1,125
  names): spells/monsters/items/deities recognised in transcripts.
  Registration follows **evidence**, not vocabulary luck (see audit below).
- **Craig multitrack support** (`--craig`, `scripts/relabel_transcript.py`):
  one audio track per speaker means speaker-labelled transcripts with no
  diarization; `mapping.json` turns Discord usernames into character names.
- **Game dictionary** (`transcripts_ai/dictionary.py` +
  `integrations/discord_game_dictionary.py`): `/game_dictionary
  lookup|fix|undo|recent` for Discord. One table-side fix is at once a
  resolver upgrade, a training record, a scorecard question, and one fewer
  review item.
- **Resolver upgrades**: fuzzy matching now scores against recorded alias
  spellings and space-squashed forms ("Valadar" → Vel Nadar; "Silverspire"
  → Silver Spire). Summaries name the known cast actually mentioned.
- **Campaign-generic guarantee**: campaign knowledge lives only in memory,
  `mapping.json` and the vault. A test proves two campaigns in one database
  never cross.

## 4. Three adversarial audits (~120 agents, findings verified by execution)

Every layer was attacked by finder lenses whose findings were then
attacked by two skeptics each. Confirmed defects, all fixed with
regression tests:

- **Teacher panel (16 fixed).** Worst: the panel could bank *wrong hard
  negatives* on a single teacher's opinion, and the documented
  GPT/Claude/Gemini configs would each have been rejected by the real APIs
  (`max_tokens` vs `max_completion_tokens`, temperature, Gemini thinking
  budgets). Also: API-key material could reach written files; mid-body
  network failures could crash a paid run.
- **Overnight loop (11 fixed).** Worst: a crash mid-write left a torn line
  that made the next run *glue a paid verdict onto it and lose it*, then
  crash on every resume forever. Also added an out-dir lock, retirement of
  already-answered questions, a futile-night guard, whole-night spend.
- **5e layer + Layer 5 (22 fixed).** Everyday narration was creating
  entities — "a gust of wind snuffs the torches" made the *spell*, "the
  traveler pushes open the door" a *deity*, "our druid" a *monster*.
  Registration now needs genuinely fantasy vocabulary, or loot evidence for
  mundanely-named items. In the quiz, "rolled a 12 and took 24 damage"
  produced an answer key of **1224**, and a character who died in an
  earlier session became "never happened" bait — both would have poisoned
  the gold labels; both fixed.

13 findings across the fleets were **refuted** by the verifiers, including
"the eval's simulated reviewer inflates the curve" — the compounding
numbers above survived their own audit.
